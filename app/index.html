          About this guide

          Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 trophy list, as Exophase and PSNProfiles published it — the two agreed on every name, description and grade. Guides are written for this entry, in our own words, and cite their sources beside each trophy. Where the record is honest about uncertainty, the guide says so: there is no published definitive missable list beyond the Chapter 1 meditation session flagged by GTABase, and the cryptic trophies (Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There) are marked as best-effort readings of their official descriptions, not as confirmed facts.

          28 of the 55 trophies are hidden on PlayStation until earned. The spoiler setting hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you tick them earned — the tracker knows what they are, you do not have to see them.
